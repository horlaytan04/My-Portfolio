# Nigeria Financial Markets & Economic Indicators Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Nigeria's financial market and economic indicators using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**. The objective is to transform multiple financial datasets into an interactive dashboard that highlights market performance, monetary policy, inflation, bond yields, and stock-sector activity.

The project covers data from the **Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)**, **Nigerian Exchange (NGX)** and related Nigerian financial-market datasets.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor NGX market performance and index trends.
- Examine inflation patterns across different inflation measures.
- Track monetary policy movements, including the Monetary Policy Rate (MPR).
- Analyze 2-year and 10-year FGN bond yields and their spread.
- Compare companies, sectors, stock prices and trading activity.
- Present financial-market information through an executive-level interactive dashboard.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — initial data inspection and preparation.
- **Power BI Desktop** — data transformation, modelling, DAX analysis and dashboard development.
- **Power Query** — cleaning, transformation and preparation of the datasets.
- **DAX** — calculated measures and analytical KPIs.

## Data Preparation & Modelling

The project combines several financial datasets into a structured Power BI data model.

### Main datasets

- CBN Inflation Measures
- CBN Money Market Indicators
- FGN 2-Year and 10-Year Bond Yields
- NGX Indices
- NGX Stocks
- NGX Company List

The data preparation process included checking data types, missing values, duplicates, column quality and consistency before modelling.

The Power BI model uses fact tables for the major financial datasets and supporting dimensions such as **Date** and **Company**. Relationships were established to allow the dashboard visuals and slicers to work consistently across the report.

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Data collection** — gathered the relevant Nigerian financial-market datasets.
2. **Data inspection** — reviewed structure, data types, missing values and duplicates.
3. **Data cleaning** — standardized and prepared the datasets for analysis.
4. **Data modelling** — created relationships between fact and dimension tables.
5. **DAX analysis** — developed measures for KPIs and market analysis.
6. **Dashboard development** — created five interactive Power BI report pages.
7. **Visual design** — applied consistent formatting, colours, slicers and navigation.
8. **Quality checks** — reviewed calculations, filters, relationships and dashboard behaviour.
